_Common Conditions_ 

- **Regimen for New cases** : This regimen is prescribed to all new pulmonary (smear positive and negative) and extra pulmonary tuberculosis. The regimen is **_2H3R3Z3 E3 / 4 H3R3_ .** 

- **Regimen for Previously Treated cases** : This regimen is prescribed for TB patients who have had more than one month anti-tuberculosis treatment previously. These patients are at a higher risk of having drug resistance. Hence, 5 drugs are prescribed in the intensive phase and the total duration of treatment is 8 months. Relapses, treatment after Default, Failures and Others are treated with this regimen. The regimen is _2S3H3R3Z3 E3 / 1H3R3Z3 E3 / 5 H3R3 E3_ .
