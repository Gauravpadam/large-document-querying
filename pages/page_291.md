_Obstetrics and Gynecology_ 

###### **Emergency contraception** 

Method used to prevent pregnancy after a likely fertile unprotected act of sexual intercourse; can be used in cases of condom rupture, rape or other circumstances of unprotected sex. First dose should be taken within 72 hours following unprotected sex and second dose 12 hours after the first dose. Any of the following can be used: 

Levonorgestrel 0.75 mg 1 tablet 12 hourly for 2 doses. Or combined oral contraceptive pills containing 50 meg ethinyl oestradiol with 0.5 mg norgestrel 2 tablets 12 hourly for 2 doses. Or  Low dose combined oral contraceptive pills containing 30 mcg ethinyl oestradiol with 0.3 mg norgestrel 4 tablets 12 hourly for 2 doses. 

###### **Patient education** 

- Side effects are nausea, vomiting, dizziness, fatigue, headache, lower abdominal pain, 

- breast tenderness, vaginal bleeding. 

- It must be used under medical supervision. 

- It decreases risk of pregnancy by 70 - 90%. Earlier it is taken, success is more. 

- If vomiting occurs within 2 hours of the dose, it must be repeated. 

- There are no contraindications for emergency contraception. 

- After this use a barrier method for each act of intercourse until next menstrual period. 

- If period delayed >5 days, rule out pregnancy. 

- Use regular contraceptive method.
