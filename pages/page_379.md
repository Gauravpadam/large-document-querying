###### **Immunization in special circumstances** 

- Immunization in preterm infants _:_ In general, all vaccines may be administered as per schedule according to the chronological age irrespective of birth weight or period of gestation. Very low birth weight/ preterm babies can be given immunization if they are stable otherwise. 

- Children receiving corticosteroids _:_ Children receiving oral corticosteroids in high doses (prednisolone 1-2 mg/kg/day) for more than 14 days should not receive live virus vaccines until the steroid has been discontinued for at least one month. Killed vaccines are safe but may not be completely effective in such situations. Patients on topical or inhaled steroids should not be denied their age appropriate vaccine. 

- Children awaiting splenectomy _:_ Immunization with pneumococcal, Hib and meningococcal vaccine should be initiated a few weeks prior to splenectomy.
